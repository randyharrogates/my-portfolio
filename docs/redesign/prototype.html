About

I'm an AI software engineer in Singapore. I build AI-native products, AI-driven workflows and multi-agent systems, and I build them with coding agents. I care about the parts that make AI trustworthy in production: citations, judges, approvals and audit trails.

Before software I spent seven years as a regular in the Singapore Army, leading and training teams. Since 2019 I have built platforms for small businesses, logistics systems, a nation-wide public healthcare application at NCS, clinical NLP at AI Singapore, and AI products in fintech. I now lead technology and AI at The Assembly Place.

How I work

AI-native from the first commit: coding agents do most of the typing; I do the planning, the reviewing and the owning.

Plan before code

Claude reads the codebase and writes a plan with concrete steps before any agent touches a file. The plan is what I review first.

Loops until green

Codex implements in its own git worktree and keeps going until the tests and checks pass. Reviewer agents send gaps back.

People own the merge

Nothing ships without me reading the diff. Shared standards, skills and hooks steer every agent the same way.

~4 → ~20releases a month after I moved a team onto this way of working.
